Nobles — Privacy Policy

Updated 24 October 2026

What Nobles collects

Nothing. The Nobles app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs that gather information.

What stays on your device

Your game in progress and your settings are saved only on your device so you can continue where you left off. Deleting the app deletes them.

Network

The app works fully offline and makes no network requests.

The website

This website, noblesthegame.com, uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages people use. Google Analytics sets cookies and receives your IP address and basic device information; it does not receive your name or email. The iPhone app does not include Google Analytics. You can opt out with Google's browser add-on.

Children

Because no data is collected, the app is suitable for all ages under applicable privacy laws.
