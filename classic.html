Quick Reference

Win Conditions

First to reach any one of these wins:

Population 15Total pop from all your holdings
Income 36Gold you collect per turn (fiefs + population). 34 in a two-player game.
ConquestCapture any enemy castle — the game ends at once

Population and Income are checked at the end of each round. Conquest is instant.
If two houses cross the line in the same round, the one further past it wins.

The Board

Every house begins in its own home province (castle must be placed there). There is one province per house and no neutral middle: provinces touch, so your neighbours are rivals from the first turn. Every house starts the same distance from its neighbours.
Initiative rotates: whoever went first this round goes last next round.

Holdings & Income

HoldingPopCostNotes
Castle5—Starting base · supply anchor
Village320gUpgrade from Outpost · supply anchor
Outpost120gReduces supply cost · NOT a supply anchor

Income per turn = Fiefs (1g each) + Population (1g per pop)
Example: 6 fiefs + Castle(5) + Village(3) = 14g/turn

Combat (Rock-Paper-Scissors)

Spearman ▲ kills Knight ◆ · Knight ◆ kills Bowman ● · Bowman ● kills Spearman ▲
Same-type ties kill both — unless defender is on Forest, then defender wins.
Bowman vs Bowman at range: both die. Bowman vs Knight at range: no effect.

Supply Lines

Path: traces ONLY through your own fiefs — unclaimed tiles block just like enemy tiles.
Cost: 1g/tile to nearest holding (Outposts count). Paid when you click End Turn.
Survival: must reach a Castle or Village. Outpost-only = unit dies.
Quartermaster / paid passage let you cross enemy fiefs (not unclaimed).

Siege & Capture

Damage ticks at end of round: besieger on the holding + every adjacent enemy unit = 1 dmg each.
Bowmen contribute unless they fired their ranged attack elsewhere this round.
Fighting your way to the adjacent tile still counts — position matters, not how you got there.
Heals +1/round when no enemy unit is on it or next to it.
Walls at 0 + your unit on it = captured at once.

Your Castle Is the Game

A castle has 5 pop of walls. Siege it to 0 with your unit inside and it is captured — you win on the spot.
Watch the Keep bar for every house: when someone's walls are nearly down, the next enemy unit to walk in ends the game.
Recruiting at a besieged castle fights the besieger immediately (rock-paper-scissors) — the last line of defence.

Building

Outpost (20g): your unit on your fief + 1-tile spacing.
Village (20g): upgrade an Outpost — select the outpost, click upgrade.
Units: one per holding per round, placed at any of your holdings (castle/village/outpost).

Movement Special Cases

Forest ends movement (except during Forced March). River crossing ends movement. Mountain/Lake impassable (lakes freeze during Winter — walk on them).
Knights conquer tiles they pass through — 2-hex move = 2 fiefs claimed.
